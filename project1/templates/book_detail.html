About This Book

Wren Halloway draws maps for a living — city planners, film sets, the occasional fantasy novelist. She's careful never to draw anything that isn't already there. Then a client hands her a torn, hand-inked chart of a coastline that matches nowhere on Earth, and asks her to finish it. The moment she does, the ink bleeds off the page and into the world around her: streets fold, doors open onto rooms that shouldn't exist, and the coastline she completed starts showing up at the edge of her own city. To close the gap she opened, Wren has to travel into the map itself, tracing its unfinished edges before whatever lives on the other side finishes them for her. Part travelogue, part unraveling mystery, The Midnight Cartographer is a novel about the places we draw to make sense of the ones we can't.
